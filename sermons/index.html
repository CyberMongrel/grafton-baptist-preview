Ways to listen

At home or on the go

Do I need Spotify Premium?

No. Our sermons are intended to be available as a free podcast. Spotify Free users can listen and download podcast episodes for offline listening in the Spotify app.

Can I download a recording?

In Spotify, use the episode's download option. That saves it inside the app, not as a separate MP3 file.

Where an “Open / download audio” link is provided here, it opens the hosted audio file in a new tab. Use your browser's download or “Save audio as” option to keep a copy. Available options depend on your browser and the audio host.

Can I listen without a Spotify account?

Where a direct audio file is available, use “Listen here” or the audio-file link. Those options do not require a Spotify account. They will only appear for recordings with a direct audio link.

Can I use another podcast app?

When our public RSS feed is available, it will be linked above. You can add it to a podcast app that supports subscriptions by feed URL. Availability in Apple Podcasts and other directories depends on the church submitting the show there.

When does this page connect to Spotify?

Only when you choose to load its player or open a Spotify link. The site does not contact Spotify just because you visit this page. Direct audio playback connects to the recording's host when you choose to listen.

Can I get a transcript or help accessing a sermon?

A transcript link will be included where one is available. For another format or help finding a particular message, contact the church.
