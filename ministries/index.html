Beyond Grafton

A wider concern

Our church has a long history of supporting mission and practical care beyond our town. The anniversary booklet records continuing child sponsorship through Baptist World Aid and involvement in Samaritan's Purse shoebox projects.

Contact us for current opportunities. Our longer-term vision includes developing a clear mission strategy and a mission trip.
