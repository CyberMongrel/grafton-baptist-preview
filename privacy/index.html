Using this website

Privacy & accessibility

A simple site, designed to be useful without asking for your personal information.

Your information

This website has no contact form, account system, advertising or site analytics. The site's own code does not set cookies or store your details in your browser. Theme choices apply to the current browsing session through the page address, not browser storage.

When the site is hosted, the hosting provider processes the technical information needed to serve pages, such as IP addresses and request information. Hosting for this site is prepared for Cloudflare Pages. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its practices.

Phone, email & external links

Phone links open your device's calling application. If an email address is listed, an email link opens your own email application. Directions links take you to Google Maps, whose own privacy terms apply. Nothing is sent to those services just by viewing our pages.

If you contact the church directly, the information you choose to share is handled through that conversation or correspondence, not collected by this website. Ask the church about its wider information-handling practices.

Sermons & Spotify

When a Spotify player is available, it is not loaded until you choose “Load Spotify player”. That action connects to Spotify, which receives technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies or browser storage under its own privacy policy. You can use an external Spotify link instead.

Choosing “Listen here” or opening a direct audio link connects to the recording's hosting service. No recording is downloaded automatically just because you visit a sermon page. Podcast feeds and transcript links also connect to their hosts when opened.

Access for everyone

The site supports keyboard navigation, zoom, reduced motion and light or dark colour themes. Text remains readable without downloaded fonts. Main information and navigation work without JavaScript.

Use the colour-theme button in the navigation to switch between light and dark. Your device preference is used on your first visit.

Visiting the building

An accessible entrance ramp was added in 2023. Contact the church about your particular access needs, toilet facilities or hearing support before visiting. Historical mentions of facilities are not a guarantee that a particular service is currently available.

Questions

For a website concern, accessibility issue or a correction to the church's history, call (02) 6642 2503.
