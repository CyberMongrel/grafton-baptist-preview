A warm welcome

Life together.
Faith in Jesus.

We're ordinary people learning to follow an extraordinary Saviour.

Some of us have been part of church all our lives. Others are just beginning to explore what they believe. We gather to worship, open the Bible, pray, and encourage one another in everyday life.

Our purpose is simple: to know and glorify God. We'd love you to be part of the story.

What we believe
01

Word

We treasure God's Word - our foundation for preaching, teaching, studying, and living.

02

Prayer

We want to be a praying people - shaped by personal and corporate prayer, seeking His presence and power.

03

Family

We are a warm, welcoming, and hospitable family - loving, serving and encouraging one another.

04

Helping

We want to be a church that listens and responds to our community - meeting needs with mercy and compassion.

Meet our pastor

Hi, I'm Tim.

It's a privilege to be part of what God is doing here in Grafton.

Tim McManus has served as our pastor since July 2024. His heart for this church is to see people come to know Jesus and grow as His disciples.

“My prayer is that faithfulness would mark not just my time here, but the whole life of this congregation, as we continue to glorify God.”

Rev. Timothy McManus · 150th anniversary booklet
