The faith we share

Our beliefs

The good news of Jesus, grounded in Scripture and lived out together.

The gospel

Good news
for all of us.

God made us to know Him. In Jesus, He has opened the way home.

Every person is made in God's image, with dignity and worth. Yet we have all turned from Him, and sin has broken our relationship with God.

Jesus Christ, God's Son, lived without sin, died for us and rose again. Forgiveness and new life are His gift, received by turning to Him and trusting Him, not earned by our achievements.

We want to help people understand this good news and learn what it means to follow Jesus.

Christian convictions

What we believe

01

The Bible

The Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired, trustworthy and authoritative Word of God, our final guide for belief and life.

02

One God

There is one true God, eternally Father, Son and Holy Spirit, perfect in love, holiness and power.

03

Jesus Christ

Jesus is fully God and fully human: born of a virgin, sinless, crucified for our sin, raised bodily from the dead, ascended and coming again.

04

Humanity & sin

Every person is made in God's image. All have sinned and fall short of His glory; we cannot save ourselves.

05

Salvation by grace

Salvation is God's gift, received through faith in Jesus Christ alone. Those who trust Him are born again.

06

The Holy Spirit

The Spirit gives new birth, lives in every believer, produces His fruit in us and equips the church for worship, holiness and witness.

07

The Church

The Church is the body of Christ. As a local family of believers we gather to worship, grow, care for one another and share Jesus.

08

Baptism & communion

We practise believer's baptism by immersion and the Lord's Supper, the two ordinances Jesus gave His church.

09

Our future hope

Jesus will return in glory. There will be resurrection and judgement, and God will make all things new.

10

Our mission

Jesus sends His people to make disciples, baptising and teaching, sharing His love in word and deed.

Baptist convictions

Faith freely lived

We value personal faith in Jesus, believer's baptism, the authority of Scripture and the freedom of each person to respond to God without coercion.

Every believer can approach God through Christ and take part in His mission. As a local congregation, we seek Christ's direction together, led and served by our pastor.

Life in the Spirit

Dependent on God

We depend on the Holy Spirit for the life and strength of our church. We welcome His gifts, tested by Scripture and exercised in order and love, to build up others.

We pursue the Spirit's fruit: love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control.
